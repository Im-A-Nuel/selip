The problem

Paying someone on-chain still scares the people you want to pay.

To receive crypto today, a normal person must make a wallet, save a seed phrase, learn about gas, and pick the right chain. Most give up. The gift never lands.

Seed phrases

One screenshot from losing everything.

Gas & chains

Friction

An app to install before they get a cent.

The solution

Give a gift, as easy as a text.

The sender builds a gift in two minutes and shares one link. The recipient signs in with Google and the gift is already theirs, claimed in one tap. The words wallet, chain and gas never appear.

Under the hood

Invisible by design.

Magic

Embedded wallet from a Google login. No seed phrase, ever.

Particle UA

EIP-7702 upgrades the wallet on first claim. Fund from any chain.

ZeroDev

Refund & time-lock rules enforced on-chain, not a backend.

Arbitrum

GiftEscrow holds value & rules. Non-custodial settlement.

Why it is different

The rules live in the contract,
not a database.

✓ Value & rules on-chain. The gift amount and its refund / lock rule sit in the smart account, not our server.
✓ Enforced by code. Refund-if-unclaimed is a ZeroDev permission, impossible for us to override.
✓ Non-custodial. No private keys, no funds ever touch the backend. The database only holds gift metadata.
✓ Walletless onboarding. The recipient's account is created and upgraded on the fly, the first time they claim.
